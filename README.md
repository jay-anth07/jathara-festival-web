# 🔱 Sri Grama Devatha Jathara 2026 — Digital Invitation Website

An authentic, culturally rich digital festival invitation website created to invite friends and guests home for our **Village Grama Devatha Jathara (Tirunalla)**. 

Our village festival has proudly been recognized as an official **Andhra Pradesh State Festival (రాష్ట్ర పండుగ)** for the past 2 years. This website combines temple heritage, festive traditions, village carnival excitement, and personal home hospitality.

## ✨ Features

- **🏛️ State Festival Recognition:** Prominently highlights the 2-year milestone of being an official Andhra Pradesh State Festival.
- **📅 Detailed Utsava Schedule:**
  - **Sept 27, 2026:** *Pata Kacheri (పాట కచేరి)* & cultural folk concert kick-off.
  - **Sept 30, 2026:** *Vigraha Prathistapana of Ammavaru (విగ్రహ ప్రతిష్టాపన)* with sacred alankaram and midnight deeparadhana.
  - **Oct 01, 2026:** *Maha Gramotsavam & Royal Procession (గ్రామోత్సవం)* with street dappulu, chariot yatra, and fireworks.
  - **Daily:** Continuous Annadanam and round-the-clock homestyle feasts.
- **🎡 Town Carnival & Fair Section:** Showcases village carnival highlights including Giant Ferris Wheels (Zaint Wheels), Columbus boat rides, bustling night bazaars, street food, and folk dances.
- **🖼️ Photo Gallery:** Visual grid featuring Ammavaru's divine alankaram, temple gopuram illumination, street processions, and carnival moments.
- **📍 Dual Location Cards:**
  - **Temple Sanctum:** Darshan timings, offerings, and direct Google Maps link.
  - **Host Residence (My Home):** Accommodation details, guest parking, food arrangements, and map navigation for friends.
- **📝 Friend RSVP Form:** Pure HTML form for friends to submit their arrival dates, travel modes (Train/Bus/Bike/Car), stay duration, and special requests.
- **📱 100% Mobile Responsive:** Fully optimized layout for seamless viewing on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- **⚡ Zero JavaScript:** Built strictly with semantic HTML5 and modern CSS3 (no external JS dependencies or frameworks).
